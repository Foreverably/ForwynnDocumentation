## New to Ticketr?

Ticketr is the most complete project here, so most people start there.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Join the support server">
    [Forwynn's Refuge](https://discord.forwynn.net) is where support for Ticketr and
    Castor lives. It's also the best place to ask questions and share feedback.
  </Step>

<Step title="Invite Ticketr">
	Add the bot to your server and grant the permissions it needs. See [Getting
	Started](/ticketr/index) for the full list, plus an explanation of why each
	permission is required.
</Step>

  <Step title="Build your first panel">
    Open the [Ticketr Dashboard](https://ticketr.forwynn.net/dashboard), select your
    server, and create a category. Then post the panel with `/ticket panel #channel`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
